# Purpose

---

# Approval

Standards SHALL be approved by the Protocol Steward following technical review.

---

# Publication

Accepted standards SHALL receive permanent identifiers.

---

# Supersession

Standards SHALL NOT be modified after publication.

Updated standards SHALL supersede earlier versions.
