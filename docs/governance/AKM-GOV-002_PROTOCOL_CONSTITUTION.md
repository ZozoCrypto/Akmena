# AKM-GOV-002

# Akmena Protocol Constitution

Version: 1.0

Status: Constitutional

Authority: Constitutional

---

# Purpose

The Constitution defines the permanent guarantees of the Akmena Protocol.

These guarantees represent the fundamental properties that every implementation must preserve.

---

# Constitutional Guarantees

## Article I — Open Participation

The protocol SHALL remain open to all participants without centralized approval.

---

## Article II — Deterministic Execution

Protocol behavior SHALL be deterministic.

Equivalent inputs SHALL produce equivalent protocol outcomes.

---

## Article III — Identity Independence

Identity SHALL remain independent from wallet addresses.

Wallet replacement SHALL NOT alter identity.

---

## Article IV — Layer Independence

Lower protocol layers SHALL remain unaware of higher protocol layers.

---

## Article V — Fixed Monetary Layer

The monetary layer SHALL remain independent from governance, reputation, registry, marketplace, and organizational logic.

---

## Article VI — Settlement Finality

Completed settlement SHALL be immutable.

---

## Article VII — Reputation Integrity

Reputation SHALL NOT be transferable.

Reputation SHALL emerge from protocol activity.

---

## Article VIII — Trust

Trust SHALL be derived from verifiable evidence.

Trust SHALL NOT be assigned arbitrarily.

---

## Article IX — Specification Supremacy

Specifications define protocol behavior.

Implementations SHALL conform to specifications.

---

## Article X — Future Compatibility

Future protocol versions SHALL preserve constitutional guarantees unless a new Constitution supersedes this document.

---

# Amendment

Constitutional amendments require a major protocol revision.

---

# Effective Status

Effective upon Governance Freeze Version 1.0.
