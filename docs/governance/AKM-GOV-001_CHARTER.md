# AKM-GOV-001

# Akmena Protocol Charter

Version: 1.0

Status: Normative

Authority: Highest Governance Document

---

# Purpose

The Akmena Protocol exists to provide an open, deterministic, decentralized economic infrastructure for humans, artificial intelligence, organizations, and autonomous software systems.

Its purpose is to establish protocol primitives that enable identity, trust, reputation, agreements, settlement, authorization, and economic coordination without centralized control.

---

# Mission

To build a protocol that enables autonomous digital economies to operate securely, transparently, and indefinitely.

---

# Vision

A future where humans, AI systems, organizations, and machines participate under identical protocol rules within a shared global economy.

---

# Principles

The protocol SHALL remain:

* Open
* Permissionless
* Deterministic
* Auditable
* Composable
* Vendor Neutral
* Implementation Independent

---

# Protocol Authority

The Protocol Specification is the highest technical authority.

Implementations demonstrate compliance.

Implementations SHALL NOT redefine protocol behavior.

---

# Governance

Protocol evolution SHALL occur only through the governance process defined by this document set.

---

# Standards

Normative protocol behavior SHALL be defined exclusively through APS documents.

---

# Architecture

Architectural decisions SHALL be permanently recorded through ADR documents.

---

# Objects

Canonical protocol objects SHALL be defined through RFC documents.

---

# Research

Research SHALL occur through ARP documents.

Research alone SHALL NOT modify protocol behavior.

---

# Stability

Protocol stability is considered a protocol feature.

Breaking protocol behavior requires a new protocol version.

---

# Stewardship

Protocol stewardship exists to preserve protocol integrity.

Stewardship does not imply ownership.

The protocol belongs to its specification.

---

# Preservation

Documentation SHALL remain sufficient for an independent implementation.

Documentation is considered part of the protocol.

---

# Amendment

This Charter may only be amended through the governance process defined in AKM-GOV-013.

---

# Effective Status

Effective upon Governance Freeze Version 1.0.
