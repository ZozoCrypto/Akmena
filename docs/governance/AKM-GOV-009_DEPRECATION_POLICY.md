# AKM-GOV-009

# Akmena Deprecation Policy

Document ID: AKM-GOV-009

Version: 1.0

Status: Normative

Authority: Governance

---

# Purpose

This document defines how protocol features become deprecated.

---

# Principles

Deprecation SHALL be predictable.

Deprecation SHALL be documented.

Deprecation SHALL NOT silently alter protocol behavior.

---

# Deprecation Process

1. Public Notice

2. Specification Update

3. Replacement Guidance

4. Compatibility Period

5. Removal in Future Major Version

---

# Compatibility

Deprecated behavior SHALL remain supported throughout the published compatibility period unless a critical security issue exists.

---

# Replacement

Every deprecated feature SHOULD identify its replacement.

---

# Historical Preservation

Deprecated specifications SHALL remain archived.

Historical protocol versions SHALL remain reproducible.
