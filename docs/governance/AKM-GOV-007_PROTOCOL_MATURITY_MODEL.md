# Akmena Protocol Maturity Model

Document ID: AKM-GOV-007

Version: 1.0

Status: Normative

Authority: Governance

---

# Purpose

This document defines the maturity stages of protocol features.

---

# Levels

## Level 0 — Research

Concept under investigation.

ARP only.

---

## Level 1 — Draft

Architecture defined.

ADR exists.

---

## Level 2 — Standard

APS approved.

RFC objects defined.

---

## Level 3 — Specified

Integrated into the Protocol Specification.

---

## Level 4 — Implemented

Reference implementation completed.

---

## Level 5 — Verified

Testing complete.

Security review complete.

---

## Level 6 — Audited

Independent audit completed.

---

## Level 7 — Frozen

Included in a frozen protocol release.

---

## Level 8 — Production

Running in production.

---

# Advancement

A feature SHALL satisfy every previous level before advancing.

Skipping maturity levels is prohibited.
