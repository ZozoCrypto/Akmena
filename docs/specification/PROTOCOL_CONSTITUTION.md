# Akmena Protocol Constitution

Version 1.0

Status: Constitutional

---

# Article I — The Protocol

The Akmena Protocol is an open technical standard.

No implementation defines the protocol.

Only the specification defines the protocol.

---

# Article II — Ownership

The protocol belongs to no individual.

The protocol belongs to no company.

The protocol belongs to no blockchain.

The protocol belongs to its specification.

Implementations may be owned.

The protocol itself is not.

---

# Article III — Source of Truth

The canonical source of truth is the Protocol Specification.

If implementation and specification disagree,

the specification is correct.

---

# Article IV — Protocol Evolution

The protocol evolves exclusively through accepted Akmena Research Proposals (ARPs).

No implementation may redefine protocol behavior without first updating the specification through the ARP process.

---

# Article V — Stability

Backward compatibility shall be preserved whenever practical.

Breaking changes require a new protocol version.

Major versions shall remain permanently archived.

---

# Article VI — Security

Security is a protocol property.

No feature shall be accepted if it weakens deterministic behavior or increases unnecessary trust assumptions.

---

# Article VII — Layer Independence

Lower layers shall never depend upon upper layers.

Financial primitives shall remain application agnostic.

Trust primitives shall remain marketplace agnostic.

Identity primitives shall remain governance agnostic.

---

# Article VIII — Implementations

Reference implementations demonstrate compliance.

They do not define behavior.

Multiple independent implementations are encouraged.

---

# Article IX — Research

Every protocol feature begins as research.

Research becomes an ARP.

Accepted ARPs become standards.

Standards modify the specification.

Implementations follow.

---

# Article X — Longevity

Every protocol decision shall assume that Akmena continues to exist decades after its original authors are gone.

The protocol shall be understandable from documentation alone.

No historical context shall be required to implement it.

---

End of Constitution.
