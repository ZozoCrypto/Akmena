# BOOK VII

# Organizations

**Protocol Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Constitutional Specification

**Layer:** Organizations

**Normative:** Yes

---

# 1. Purpose

This Book defines Organizations as first-class Participants within the Akmena Protocol.

Organizations coordinate Participants to pursue shared objectives while remaining subject to protocol rules.

---

# 2. Scope

Defines:

* Organizations
* Membership
* Organizational authority
* Organizational assets
* Organizational coordination

---

# 3. Organizations

Organizations SHALL possess protocol Identity.

Organizations SHALL remain Participants.

Organizations MAY own Assets.

Organizations MAY enter Agreements.

Organizations MAY participate in Governance.

---

# 4. Membership

Membership represents association with an Organization.

Membership SHALL NOT automatically grant authority.

Authority SHALL remain explicit.

---

# 5. Organizational Authority

Organizations SHALL exercise authority only through protocol-defined authorization.

Organizational authority SHALL remain auditable.

---

# 6. Organizational Assets

Organizations MAY own Assets.

Ownership SHALL remain explicit.

Organizational ownership SHALL follow Economic Layer rules.

---

# 7. Organizational Governance

Organizations MAY define internal governance policies.

Internal governance SHALL NOT violate protocol invariants.

---

# 8. Organizational Relationships

Organizations MAY interact with:

* Participants
* Organizations
* Agreements
* Services
* Marketplaces

All relationships SHALL remain explicit.

---

# 9. Organizational Invariants

The following SHALL remain true:

* Organizations remain Participants.
* Membership does not imply authority.
* Organizational authority remains explicit.
* Organizations follow protocol governance.
