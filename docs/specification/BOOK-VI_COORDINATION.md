# BOOK VI

# Coordination

**Protocol Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Constitutional Specification

**Layer:** Coordination

**Normative:** Yes

---

# 1. Purpose

This Book defines how independent Participants coordinate activities within the Akmena Protocol.

Coordination enables collective action while preserving participant autonomy.

---

# 2. Scope

Defines:

* proposals
* voting
* delegation
* collective decision making
* coordination invariants

---

# 3. Coordination Layer

The Coordination Layer exists above Trust.

Coordination SHALL compose:

* Identity
* Economics
* Trust

Coordination SHALL NOT modify lower-layer semantics.

---

# 4. Collective Action

Participants MAY cooperate voluntarily.

The protocol SHALL provide standardized coordination mechanisms.

Participation SHALL remain voluntary.

---

# 5. Proposals

A Proposal records a proposed protocol or organizational action.

Proposals SHALL remain immutable after publication.

---

# 6. Voting

Voting records participant decisions regarding proposals.

Votes SHALL remain:

* explicit
* auditable
* immutable

---

# 7. Delegation

Delegation authorizes another Participant to exercise defined coordination rights.

Delegation SHALL be:

* explicit
* scoped
* revocable

Delegation SHALL NOT permanently transfer authority.

---

# 8. Consensus

Consensus represents the outcome of protocol-defined coordination.

Consensus SHALL emerge from protocol rules.

Consensus SHALL NOT alter constitutional principles.

---

# 9. Coordination Invariants

The following SHALL remain true:

* Participation is voluntary.
* Votes remain immutable.
* Delegation remains revocable.
* Consensus follows protocol rules.
* Coordination SHALL remain deterministic.
