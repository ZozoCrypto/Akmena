# ADR-003

# Identity Immutability

Status: Accepted

Decision Type: Identity

---

# Decision

Protocol identities are immutable.

Ownership and authorization may change.

Identity itself does not.

---

# Rationale

Stable identities provide durable references for agreements, reputation, memory, and governance.

---

# Consequences

Historical consistency

Reliable references

Long-term interoperability
