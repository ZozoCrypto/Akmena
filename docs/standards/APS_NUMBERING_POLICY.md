# APS Numbering Policy

Version: 1.0

Status: Normative

Authority: Standards

---

# Purpose

Defines permanent numbering for Akmena Protocol Standards.

---

# Numbering

APS identifiers are permanent.

Identifiers SHALL never be reused.

Withdrawn standards SHALL retain their identifiers.

---

# Categories

APS-100 Identity

APS-200 Economics

APS-300 Trust

APS-400 Organizations

APS-500 AI Economy

APS-600 Interoperability

APS-700 Governance Interfaces

APS-800 Developer Interfaces

APS-900 Security

---

# Stability

Published identifiers are immutable.

Future standards SHALL receive the next available identifier within the appropriate category.

---

# Supersession

New standards supersede previous versions.

Previous identifiers remain archived.

They are never reassigned.
