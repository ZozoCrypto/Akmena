# APS-506

# Service Standard

Version: 1.0

Status: Draft

Category: Autonomous Economy Layer

Authority: Protocol Standard

Classification: Normative

---

# Purpose

This standard defines protocol services.

A service represents executable functionality offered by a protocol participant.

Services perform work.

Agreements define commitments.

---

# Scope

This standard governs:

* service publication
* service execution
* service interfaces
* service outcomes
* service lifecycle

---

# Service Principles

Services SHALL be:

discoverable

deterministic

identity associated

capability driven

implementation independent

---

# Service Lifecycle

Defined

↓

Published

↓

Available

↓

Invoked

↓

Executed

↓

Completed

---

# Service Outcomes

Execution MAY produce:

agreements

assets

attestations

memory

settlement

additional services

---

# Normative Requirements

Services SHALL identify their provider.

Services SHALL expose supported interfaces.

Service execution SHALL preserve deterministic protocol behavior.

Execution results SHALL remain auditable.

---

# Compliance

Implementations SHALL preserve execution integrity.

---

# Security Considerations

Unauthorized execution SHALL be prevented.

Service failures SHALL preserve protocol consistency.
