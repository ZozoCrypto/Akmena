# APS-104

# Credential Standard

Version: 1.0

Status: Draft

Category: Identity & Coordination

Authority: Protocol Standard

---

# Purpose

This standard defines protocol credentials.

Credentials provide verifiable assertions associated with protocol identities.

Credentials are evidence.

Credentials are not identity.

---

# Scope

This standard governs:

* Credential issuance
* Credential ownership
* Credential verification
* Credential expiration
* Credential revocation

---

# Normative Requirements

Credentials SHALL reference a protocol identity.

Credentials SHALL remain independently verifiable.

Credentials SHALL support expiration.

Credentials SHALL support revocation.

Credentials SHALL support multiple issuers.

Credentials SHALL NOT modify identity.

---

# Credential Properties

Credentials MAY represent:

* Human verification
* Organization membership
* Professional qualifications
* AI certifications
* Capabilities
* Trust assertions

---

# Verification

Verification SHALL remain deterministic.

Verification SHALL NOT require centralized authorities.

---

# Compliance

Implementations SHALL preserve credential integrity.

---

# Security Considerations

Credential forgery SHALL be detectable.

Credential revocation SHALL remain verifiable.
