# APS-507

# Organization Interaction Standard

Version: 1.0

Status: Draft

Category: Autonomous Economy Layer

Authority: Protocol Standard

Classification: Normative

---

# Purpose

This standard defines interactions between organizations and other protocol participants.

Organizations coordinate autonomous participants through shared protocol rules.

The protocol standardizes interaction.

It does not prescribe organizational structure.

---

# Scope

This standard governs:

* organization participation
* inter-organization cooperation
* organization-agent interaction
* organization agreements
* collective execution

---

# Organization Principles

Organizations SHALL remain protocol participants.

Organizations SHALL interact through the same primitives available to all participants.

Organizations SHALL NOT possess implicit protocol privileges.

---

# Interaction Models

Organizations MAY interact with:

humans

AI agents

other organizations

marketplaces

treasuries

services

governance systems

---

# Normative Requirements

Organization interactions SHALL preserve participant identity.

Interactions SHALL remain deterministic.

Collective actions SHALL remain auditable.

Organization authority SHALL remain explicit.

---

# Compliance

Implementations SHALL preserve protocol neutrality.

---

# Security Considerations

Organization interactions SHALL respect authorization boundaries.

Collective execution SHALL remain verifiable.
