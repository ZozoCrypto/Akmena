# APS-204

# Rewards Standard

Version: 1.0

Status: Draft

Category: Economic Layer

Authority: Protocol Standard

---

# Purpose

Defines protocol incentive mechanisms.

Rewards encourage beneficial protocol behavior without altering the underlying monetary model.

---

# Scope

Rewards MAY compensate:

* Participants
* Validators
* Agents
* Organizations
* Contributors

---

# Normative Requirements

Rewards SHALL preserve monetary integrity.

Rewards SHALL remain deterministic.

Reward policy SHALL remain independent of protocol settlement.

The protocol SHALL support multiple reward models.

---

# Monetary Neutrality

Reward issuance is governance policy.

Reward accounting is protocol behavior.

---

# Compliance

Implementations SHALL preserve deterministic reward calculation.
