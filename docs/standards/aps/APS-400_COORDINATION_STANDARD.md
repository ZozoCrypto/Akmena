# APS-400

# Coordination Standard

Version: 1.0

Status: Draft

Category: Coordination Layer

Authority: Protocol Standard

Classification: Normative

---

# Purpose

This standard defines the coordination primitives of the Akmena Protocol.

Coordination enables independent protocol participants to cooperate toward shared objectives without requiring centralized authority.

The protocol coordinates participants.

It does not govern them.

---

# Scope

This standard governs:

* autonomous cooperation
* collective coordination
* organizational interaction
* shared objectives
* protocol coordination primitives

---

# Coordination Principles

Coordination SHALL be:

* deterministic
* permission-aware
* identity-based
* implementation independent
* extensible

Coordination SHALL NOT require trust.

Coordination SHALL NOT require hierarchy.

Coordination SHALL NOT require centralized ownership.

---

# Participants

Coordination MAY occur between:

* Humans
* AI Agents
* Organizations
* Treasuries
* Protocol Services
* Future autonomous entities

All participants SHALL be treated as protocol peers.

---

# Shared Objectives

Participants MAY coordinate around:

* agreements
* governance
* marketplaces
* resource allocation
* service execution
* collective ownership

---

# Normative Requirements

Coordination SHALL preserve participant autonomy.

Coordination SHALL preserve protocol determinism.

Coordination SHALL remain independent of implementation.

Coordination SHALL support future coordination models.

---

# Compliance

Implementations SHALL preserve deterministic coordination behavior.

---

# Security Considerations

Coordination SHALL resist unauthorized influence.

Coordination SHALL preserve participant independence.
