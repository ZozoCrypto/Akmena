# APS-200

# Monetary Layer Standard

Version: 1.0

Status: Draft

Category: Economic Layer

Authority: Protocol Standard

---

# Purpose

This standard defines the monetary foundation of the Akmena Protocol.

The protocol governs the movement and coordination of value. It does not prescribe monetary policy.

---

# Scope

This standard governs:

* Value
* Assets
* Monetary representation
* Conservation of value
* Economic neutrality

---

# Economic Principles

The monetary layer SHALL be:

* Deterministic
* Technology neutral
* Asset agnostic
* Extensible
* Conservational

---

# Economic Primitives

## Value

Value represents transferable economic worth recognized by the protocol.

## Asset

An identifiable representation of value.

## Monetary Representation

The protocol MAY support multiple representations of value.

AKM is one such representation.

Future protocol versions MAY introduce additional representations.

---

# Normative Requirements

The protocol SHALL preserve conservation of value.

The protocol SHALL define monetary behavior independently of monetary policy.

The protocol SHALL support multiple asset types.

The protocol SHALL remain implementation independent.

---

# Compliance

Implementations SHALL preserve deterministic monetary behavior.

---

# Security Considerations

Unauthorized value creation SHALL be impossible.

Unauthorized value destruction SHALL be impossible.
