# APS-503

# Discovery Standard

Version: 1.0

Status: Draft

Category: Autonomous Economy Layer

Authority: Protocol Standard

Classification: Normative

---

# Purpose

Defines protocol discovery.

Discovery enables autonomous participants to locate compatible participants, services, organizations, and opportunities.

---

# Scope

Discovery governs:

participants

services

organizations

skills

marketplaces

agreements

---

# Discovery Principles

Discovery SHALL be:

identity aware

permission aware

deterministic

implementation independent

extensible

---

# Discovery Objects

Discovery MAY expose:

identities

services

skills

organizations

marketplaces

agreements

---

# Normative Requirements

Discovery SHALL preserve participant privacy where required.

Discovery SHALL support capability filtering.

Discovery SHALL remain independent of implementation.

---

# Compliance

Implementations SHALL preserve deterministic discovery behavior.

---

# Security Considerations

Discovery SHALL resist unauthorized enumeration.

Discovery SHALL respect protocol permissions.
