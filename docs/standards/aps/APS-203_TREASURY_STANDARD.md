# APS-203

# Treasury Standard

Version: 1.0

Status: Draft

Category: Economic Layer

Authority: Protocol Standard

---

# Purpose

Defines collective ownership of protocol assets.

---

# Scope

Treasuries govern:

* Collective assets
* Budget allocation
* Resource custody

---

# Normative Requirements

Treasuries SHALL own assets.

Treasuries SHALL support multiple controllers.

Treasuries SHALL preserve auditability.

Treasuries SHALL support deterministic accounting.

---

# Ownership

Treasury ownership SHALL remain distinct from controller authority.

---

# Compliance

Implementations SHALL maintain verifiable treasury balances.
