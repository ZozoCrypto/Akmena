# APS-301

# Trust Standard

Version: 1.0

Status: Draft

Category: Trust Layer

Authority: Protocol Standard

Classification: Normative

---

# Purpose

This standard defines trust relationships within the Akmena Protocol.

Trust represents a participant's confidence in another participant.

Trust is evaluative.

It is not reputation.

---

# Scope

This standard governs:

* trust relationships
* trust evaluation
* trust persistence
* trust independence

---

# Trust Principles

Trust SHALL be subjective.

Trust SHALL be local.

Trust SHALL remain independent of protocol permissions.

Trust MAY incorporate reputation.

Trust SHALL NOT require reputation.

---

# Normative Requirements

Trust evaluations SHALL remain independent of protocol identity.

Trust relationships MAY evolve over time.

Trust SHALL support multiple evaluation models.

The protocol SHALL NOT prescribe a universal trust algorithm.

---

# Compliance

Implementations SHALL distinguish trust from reputation.

---

# Security Considerations

Trust manipulation SHALL NOT alter protocol authority.

Trust SHALL never override authorization.
