# APS-201

# Settlement Standard

Version: 1.0

Status: Draft

Category: Economic Layer

Authority: Protocol Standard

---

# Purpose

Defines irreversible completion of protocol obligations.

Settlement is the mechanism by which economic commitments become final.

---

# Scope

This standard governs:

* Settlement
* Finality
* Completion
* Deterministic execution

---

# Normative Requirements

Settlement SHALL be deterministic.

Settlement SHALL be irreversible.

Settlement SHALL preserve conservation of value.

Settlement SHALL satisfy protocol obligations.

---

# Finality

Settlement establishes economic finality.

Final settlement SHALL NOT require subsequent confirmation.

---

# Compliance

Implementations SHALL guarantee deterministic settlement.

---

# Security Considerations

Settlement SHALL resist replay.

Settlement SHALL prevent double settlement.
