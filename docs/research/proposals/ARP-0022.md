# ARP-0022

# Monetary Invariants

Status: Draft

Version: 1.0

Category: Monetary Policy

Requires: ARP-0004

---

## Abstract

The monetary layer provides immutable guarantees regarding AKM.

Money is the protocol foundation.

---

## Monetary Rules

• Fixed maximum supply.

• No inflation after deployment.

• No hidden minting.

• No hidden burning.

• Ownership determined only through cryptographic authorization.

• Settlement SHALL always be final.

• Replay SHALL be impossible.

---

## Conclusion

Applications evolve.

Money remains stable.
