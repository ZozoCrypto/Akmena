# ARP-0005
## Trust Layer

Status: Accepted

---

# Purpose

Trust measures observed behavior.

Trust is earned.

Trust is never purchased.

---

# Trust Sources

Trust may derive from:

- successful agreements
- fulfilled escrow
- verified credentials
- dispute outcomes
- historical reliability

---

# Trust Requirements

Trust SHALL:

change over time

increase through successful interactions

decrease after failures

remain transparent

---

# Trust SHALL NOT

depend on token holdings

depend on wealth

depend on governance power

be manually assigned by administrators

---

# Philosophy

Trust is an emergent property.

It is not a permission.
