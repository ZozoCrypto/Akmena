# ARP-0007
## Marketplace Layer

Status: Accepted

---

# Purpose

The Marketplace matches demand with supply.

The Marketplace never owns assets.

---

# Responsibilities

The Marketplace SHALL:

publish listings

discover services

match participants

forward settlement to Escrow

---

# Marketplace SHALL NOT

hold funds

calculate reputation

verify identities

mint tokens

The Marketplace coordinates.

Escrow settles.
Marketplace SHALL NOT

execute agreements
