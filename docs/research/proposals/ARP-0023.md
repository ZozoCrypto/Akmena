# ARP-0023

# Authorization Standard

Status: Draft

Version: 1.0

Category: Authorization

Requires: ARP-0012

---

## Abstract

Authorization separates ownership from execution.

A signed intent authorizes movement of value without requiring the signer to submit the transaction.

---

## Principles

Every authorization SHALL include:

• signer

• recipient

• value

• validity window

• nonce

Replay protection SHALL always exist.

Expired authorizations SHALL fail.

Canceled authorizations SHALL fail.

---

## Conclusion

Authorization enables autonomous commerce while preserving ownership.
