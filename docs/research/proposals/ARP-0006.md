# ARP-0006
## Escrow Layer

Status: Accepted

---

# Purpose

Escrow guarantees conditional settlement.

Funds move only after agreed conditions are satisfied.

---

# Responsibilities

Escrow SHALL:

hold assets

track agreements

release funds

refund funds

record settlement

---

# Security

Escrow SHALL:

use pull payments

prevent reentrancy

prevent replay

prevent double settlement

prevent deadline manipulation

---

# State Machine

Created

↓

Funded

↓

Delivered

↓

Completed

or

↓

Refunded

Terminal states are immutable.
Escrow SHALL be deterministic.

Escrow SHALL never take custody beyond settlement requirements.
