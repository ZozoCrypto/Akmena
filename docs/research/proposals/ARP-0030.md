# ARP-0030

# The Akmena Constitution

Status: Draft

Version: 1.0

Category: Constitutional

---

## Abstract

The Constitution summarizes the immutable principles governing the Akmena Protocol.

Every future protocol decision should be evaluated against these principles.

---

## Constitutional Principles

Money before applications.

Trust before convenience.

Security before speed.

Simplicity before complexity.

Research before code.

Open standards before proprietary systems.

Humans and AI cooperate.

Ownership remains explicit.

Trust remains measurable.

Protocol layers remain independent.

---

## Final Statement

Ethereum made value programmable.

Akmena makes trust programmable.

The protocol exists to enable secure cooperation between humans, autonomous AI agents, organizations, and future intelligent systems.

These principles should remain true regardless of implementation or future protocol versions.
