# ARP-0027

# AI Economic Safety

Status: Draft

Version: 1.0

Category: AI Safety

---

## Abstract

AI agents must remain economically constrained.

Autonomy does not imply unlimited authority.

---

## Principles

Agents SHALL only spend authorized funds.

Agents SHALL respect protocol permissions.

Agents SHALL never bypass settlement.

Agents SHALL remain individually accountable.

Organizations SHALL define authority explicitly.

---

## Conclusion

Economic safety protects both humans and AI.
