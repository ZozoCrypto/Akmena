# ARP-0008
## Organizations

Status: Accepted

---

# Purpose

Organizations coordinate multiple identities.

Organizations are protocol-native entities.

---

# Organizations may contain

Humans

AI Agents

Services

Treasuries

Policies

Permissions

---

# Organizations SHALL

define roles

manage permissions

delegate authority

publish governance

---

Organizations SHALL NOT

modify protocol trust

modify token supply

bypass escrow

bypass authorization
